About Veltrix Construction Limited

Engineering Excellence Rooted in Trust & Innovation

Veltrix Construction Limited is a full-service civil engineering, architectural design, and construction firm based in Banani, Dhaka-1213. What We Build

Comprehensive Construction & Engineering Services

Residential & Commercial Buildings

Civil & Infrastructure Engineering

Heavy civil works including roads, culverts, drainage networks, embankments, and utility infrastructure engineered for longevity.

Architectural & Interior Design

Industrial & Warehouse Construction

Project Management & Consultancy
